### StoryDraft

The buyer's story as a short paragraph the buyer corrects in place. Write two to four sentences in the second person from what the buyer said, and put a slot where a value belongs: the commute mode, the longest trip, a thing that matters, or a place they travel to. Mark a slot assumed when you guessed it. The buyer changes slots on the device; you never write their values. Bind story to /story.

### Hunch

One deduction about the buyer, the clue it came from, and a tap to confirm or correct it. The first option confirms the hunch; the others are the likeliest alternatives. The app adds facet, value, and label to the answer action. Values by facet: place, a map query without the city; mode, walk, bike, car, or transit; maxMinutes and rooms, a whole number; homeType, flat or house; outdoor, none, balcony, or garden; style, classic or modern; criterion, a criterion kind. A place option with pin true opens the map to drop a pin instead of its value. Bind story to /story.

### PhotoLineup

Four photos from the buyer's city, of homes or of ways to live there, curated with the traits each stands for. The buyer taps the one that feels like theirs, and the server reads its traits. Bind photos to a lineup the turn context lists. The app adds facet photo, the photo id as value, and its caption as label to the pick action.

### HomeSwipe

Six real sample homes from the buyer's city, one card at a time, that the buyer keeps or passes with a swipe or a tap. The server chose them to differ in kind, outdoor space, style, and size. Bind homes to /swipe/homes. After the last card the app adds facet swipe, the kept ids as value, a label, and the kept and passed lists to the answer action.

### TradeOff

Two or three ways to live side by side, as compare_areas priced them: an area and its character, a home type and size, the estimated price from the area's price per square metre with its source, and the minutes to their daily place. The buyer picks one, which sets the area and a price range. Call compare_areas first and bind options to /tradeoff/options. The app adds facet area, the option id as value, and a label to the pick action.

### Routine

The buyer's week as taps: every place they go regularly with how many days a week, those the case knows already filled in, and a way to add another, such as a clinic once a week, by name and a pin on the map. Bind routine to /story/routine and story to /story, beside a Button sending answer with the facet routine and storyPath /story.

### LifestyleCommuteMap

A map of the buyer's life around the homes they weigh: the places they travel to, each home, the route from each home to each place with its minutes and how many days a week they make the trip, and the nearest place for each criterion. Before homes are measured, bind markers to /story/markers; after, bind markers to /assessment/markers and routes to /assessment/routes. With focus on one home it shows their week from there. Its properties follow the Maps Agentic UI Toolkit GoogleMap.

### ComparisonTable

Every criterion against every home: whether it fits, comes close and misses by how much, or misses, with what was measured. Must-haves are marked. Bind rows to /assessment/rows and homes to /assessment/candidates.

### DynamicRecommendationStack

The buyer's homes ranked by how well they fit the story, best first: what fits, what comes close and by how much, what misses, and whether a must-have is lost, each with a button to choose that home. Bind homes to /assessment/candidates. choose is sent with the chosen home added to its context as candidateId.

### ListingCard

One sample home in full: photos to swipe through, price, size, rooms, and floor, the price per square metre against its area's median with the source, what fits and what misses, and a button to choose it. Labelled as a sample listing. Bind homes to /assessment/candidates and name the home by id. choose is sent with candidateId added.

### LocationPicker

Chooses a market or a country and writes its ISO 3166 code. Use mode market for where the buyer is buying, with the select_market action; use mode country for citizenship and tax residence.

### MoneyInput

An amount field that writes a money object to the bound path. Use it for incomes, savings, debts, and fees; never write amounts yourself.

### HouseholdInput

Asks who lives in the home: how many adults and the age of each child, with a one-tap answer for no children. In the facts form bind it to /profile/adults and /profile/childAges; when the case asks who moves, bind it to /story/adults and /story/childAges beside a Button sending answer with the facet household and storyPath /story.

### AmountSlider

A money slider between bounds computed from the rules, showing the value and its share of another amount. Use it for the down payment, bound to /derived/downPaymentBounds.

### MarketSnapshotCard

The selected market at a glance for this buyer: its name, whether Homeward has curated rules there, the kinds of rule that apply, and the reference rate behind the payment. In an uncovered market it says so and names the markets Homeward covers. Draw it when the market is new to the buyer.

### AffordabilitySummary

The maximum loan, the rule that limits it, and headroom against the current scenario, computed from the rules. Show it after the profile is committed; never restate its figures in text.

### PaymentBreakdown

The first monthly payment split into interest and amortization, with fees, the total, and in Sweden the cost after the interest deduction.

### PurchaseCostsTable

Every upfront cost this buyer owes on the day, the total, and the cash needed with the down payment. A charge that is partly refunded shows what comes back and what earns it.

### StressTestChart

Monthly cost from today's rate up past the rate the lender tests, against the most this buyer can pay each month, with today and the stress rate marked.

### EligibilityChecklist

Every lending rule this buyer is measured against, each passing, warning, or failing, with what would settle it and what facts are still missing.

### ForeignBuyerObstacles

What stands between this buyer and the deed, grouped by whether it blocks the purchase, costs effort, or only corrects an expectation, each with what to do about it and how long it takes, and the deadline of any tax a refund depends on.

## Data Model

### `/story`

Why and where the buyer is moving, what they care about, and the homes or areas they are weighing. StoryDraft writes the answer fields as the buyer corrects the story, and commit_story folds them into the story and confirms it. anchors, candidates, criteria, and center are read-only and placed by the server.

| Path | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/story/reason` | one of work, study, family, lifestyle, retirement, investment, other | Why the buyer is moving. |
| `/story/city` | string | Where they are moving to. |
| `/story/mode` | one of walk, bike, car, transit | How they will travel to their anchors. |
| `/story/maxMinutes` | integer | The longest trip to an anchor they accept, in minutes. |
| `/story/picks/schools` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria picked for schools and care. |
| `/story/picks/everyday` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria picked for everyday. |
| `/story/picks/activities` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria picked for activities. |
| `/story/picks/evenings` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria picked for evenings. |
| `/story/picks/gettingAround` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria picked for getting around. |
| `/story/picks/downsides` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria picked for downsides. |
| `/story/mustHaves` | list of criterion kinds, such as ["schools", "parks"] | Criteria the buyer will not give up. |
| `/story/adults` | integer | Adults moving, for a HouseholdInput asking who moves. |
| `/story/childAges` | list of integers | Age of each child moving, for a HouseholdInput asking who moves. |

| `/story/center` | object read by Homeward components | Where the map opens: the city as lat and lng. |

| `/story/markers` | object read by Homeward components | Map markers for the city, the anchors, and the homes placed so far, for a Map before the assessment exists. |
| `/story/anchors` | object read by Homeward components | Places the buyer travels to: id, label, mode, maxMinutes, point. |
| `/story/candidates` | object read by Homeward components | Homes or areas being weighed: id, label, point, price. |
| `/story/criteria` | object read by Homeward components | What is weighed: kind, anchorId, mustHave. |

| --- | --- | --- |
| `/swipe/homes` | object read by Homeward components | Six listings that differ in kind, outdoor space, style, and size. |

### `/tradeoff`

The ways to live compare_areas priced, read-only. Bind a TradeOff to it.

| Path | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/tradeoff/options` | object read by Homeward components | Each option: id, area, character, home type, rooms, size, estimated price, the price per square metre with its source, and minutes to the first placed anchor. |

### `/assessment`

How each home fits each criterion, measured by the server, read-only. Bind building blocks to its lists.

| Path | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/assessment/candidates` | object read by Homeward components | Each home with rank, score, missesMustHave, and pros, cons, and close as ready sentences; a sample home carries its listing. |
| `/assessment/rows` | object read by Homeward components | One row per criterion: label, group, mustHave, and a cell per home with status fits, close, misses, or unknown, text, and detail. |
| `/assessment/markers` | object read by Homeward components | Map markers for anchors, homes, and the nearest places found. |
| `/assessment/routes` | object read by Homeward components | A route from each home to each anchor with its minutes and path. |

### `/scenario`

The purchase being tried. Inputs write one field each; insights bind the whole slice.

| Path | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/scenario/price` | money object { "minorUnits": integer, "currency": ISO 4217 code } | Price of the home. |
| `/scenario/downPayment` | money object { "minorUnits": integer, "currency": ISO 4217 code } | Cash the buyer puts down. |
| `/scenario/termYears` | integer | Loan term in years. |
| `/scenario/loanTypeId` | string | A loan type id from the market, such as variable-3m. |
| `/scenario/rateSpreadPpm` | integer parts per million; 10000 is 1% | Lender's margin over the reference rate. |
| `/scenario/rateShockPpm` | integer parts per million; 10000 is 1% | Rate rise to test against. |
| `/scenario/associationFee` | money object { "minorUnits": integer, "currency": ISO 4217 code } | Monthly fee to the housing association. |

### `/market`

The rules of the selected market that apply to this buyer, read-only. Insights bind the whole slice.

| Path | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/market/id` | ISO 3166 code of a country or subdivision, such as SE or PT-30 | The market. |
| `/market/name` | string | Market name in English. |
| `/market/coverage` | one of curated, uncovered | Whether Homeward has curated rules here. An uncovered market carries only the market, its name, and this field. |
| `/market/currency` | ISO 4217 currency code | Currency of prices and loans; bind it as the currency of money inputs. |

### `/derived`

Figures the server computed for the scenario, read-only.

| Path | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/derived/affordability` | object read by Homeward components | Maximum loan, the binding limit, and each check. |
| `/derived/downPaymentBounds` | { "min", "max", "step" }, each a money object | Down payment range the rules allow; bind it as AmountSlider bounds. |
| `/derived/payment` | object read by Homeward components | First monthly interest, amortization, and total. |
