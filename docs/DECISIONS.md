# Homeward Decisions

The record of what was decided and what was weighed against it, from the
planning session on 2026-09-16 onwards. A row that a later decision replaced
stays as history: D129, the reset of 2026-09-26, replaced most rows before it,
and D173 to D175, the cleanup of 2026-10-08, replaced the rows about accounts,
Firestore, and the plans list. `docs/SPIKES.md` holds the evidence behind the
rows that name a spike.
